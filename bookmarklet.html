🎯 Game Rules

  • Board: 91 hexagons in Gliński's hexagonal chess layout
  • Pawns: Move forward, capture diagonally. Center pawn cannot move 2 spaces initially.
  • Castling: Modified for hexagonal board - king replaces rook/knight position
  • En Passant: Not available due to center pawn rule modification
  • Objective: Checkmate the opponent's king

Built with pure JavaScript • No server required • Based on Gliński's hexagonal chess
